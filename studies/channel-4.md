## 01 — Introduction

> **Writing prompt — delete this block when done:** Introduce the channel in your own words. What kind of channel is it? Why did you pick it for this study? How long did you watch it — days, weeks? What were you generally paying attention to?

## 02 — Identity & Branding

> **Writing prompt — delete this block when done:** Describe the channel's identity: the logo, the colour palette, the typography of its promos, its taglines and audio stings. How consistent is all of it? What feeling is the brand going for — and does it land?

## 03 — Content & Programming

> **Writing prompt — delete this block when done:** Break down what actually airs: flagship shows, weekday vs weekend schedules, repeat slots, filler programming. When is prime time and what carries it? Note any scheduling patterns or tricks you spot.

## 04 — On-Screen Presentation

> **Writing prompt — delete this block when done:** The on-screen layer: channel bug / watermark placement, promo style, ad-break packaging, lower thirds, show name straps, transitions. What feels polished? What feels dated or cluttered?

## 05 — What I Learned

> **Writing prompt — delete this block when done:** Your takeaways — first as a viewer, then as a front-end developer. Did anything here change how you'd approach branding, layout or motion in your own work? Keep it honest and specific.
